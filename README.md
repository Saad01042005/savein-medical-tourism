# SaveIN.Life — Medical Tourism Market Entry Strategy

A comprehensive case study on market entry strategy for a healthcare technology startup in India's fragmented medical tourism space.

## What This Is

This is a professional portfolio case study showcasing end-to-end strategic consulting work. It documents the research, analysis, and business model design executed for SaveIN.Life — an early-stage Medical Value Travel Facilitator (MVTF) startup.

**Delivered under:** 180 Degrees Consulting, NSUT

## What's Covered

- **Competitor Analysis** — Profiled 10+ national and 5 international MVTFs across services, revenue models, customer channels, and digital presence
- **Legal & Regulatory Landscape** — Mapped NABH/JCI accreditation processes, government schemes, key stakeholders, and forecasted future regulations
- **Business Model Design** — Full Business Model Canvas with 14 prioritised service offerings, 7 revenue streams, and clear go-to-market strategy
- **Sales Funnel Strategy** — 4-stage customer acquisition funnel (Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Conversion & Loyalty)
- **Market Insights** — Quantitative analysis of 94 MVTFs identifying survival patterns, failure rates, and competitive gaps

## Key Findings

- **56% of MVTFs fail** — but funded startups survive 37% longer (9.7 vs 7.1 years)
- **No dominant player** — top 3 funded companies serve <15% of annual inbound medical tourists, creating a market entry opportunity
- **68% of patients come from Bangladesh** — not Western markets; this shapes language, logistics, and marketing strategy
- **Dedicated Patient Manager is the #1 USP** — nearly no competitor offers this service, but it's the clearest trust signal for international patients
